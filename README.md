# Tuttly

Tuttly is a language-learning app being developed into a personal AI tutor,
using saved vocabulary and learning material as context for lessons and
conversations.

**Current status:** Vocabulary capture and storage are available. AI tutoring
and lesson generation are planned.

## Available now

- Capture vocabulary from photographed pages.
- Choose words and save their translations.
- Organise your vocabulary by collection.

## Feature roadmap

- **AI tutor conversations.** Practise everyday situations and talk about topics that interest you.
- **Personalised vocabulary lessons.** Work on words and expressions from your own collection.
- **Guided practice with saved words.** Recall vocabulary and use it to express your own ideas.
